# FACTS LEDGER (external facts)

Under Golden Rule 2 and decisions D2/D12, every fact the dataset can't supply is either **VERIFIED** (with its source) or tagged **[ASSUMPTION]** (with a one-line reason). Raw evidence from each Facts Researcher package is kept in `data/work/facts/`. This ledger is the consolidated view, maintained by the supervisor.

Status values:
- **VERIFIED (official)**: confirmed on an official IPL or BCCI page.
- **VERIFIED (secondary, 2 sources)**: two independent reputable sources agree.
- **VERIFIED (news)**: an auction or squad fact confirmed by reputable reporting, with a recheck due.
- **UNKNOWN**: could not be verified; never guessed.
- **[ASSUMPTION]**: a stated assumption, with its reason.

Fact checks:
- **F1**: Step 0.
- **F2**: before the Step 5 recommendations.
- **F3**: before final delivery.
